# TKT-3103 — Verdemar Offshore Systems — site survey outcome

- operator: OP-2745
- status: closed
- opened: 2026-09-01
- closed: 2026-09-07

Verdemar Offshore Systems (applicant reference OP-2745) applied for a two-unit maritime installation.

The RF site survey failed: co-channel interference from an adjacent installation exceeded the limits in the commissioning standard. The application was declined on 2026-09-07 and no equipment was shipped.

Verdemar is not an operator on the platform and has no units, no support plan and no service cases. They may reapply once the interference is resolved.
