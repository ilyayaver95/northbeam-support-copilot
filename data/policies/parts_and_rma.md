# Spare Parts, Returns and Advance Replacement

## Lead times

| Part class | Examples | Stocked lead time | Non-stocked |
|---|---|---|---|
| Line-replaceable unit (LRU) | transmitter module, receiver front end, PSU | 2 business days | 15 business days |
| Rotating assembly | motor, azimuth encoder, slip ring | 5 business days | 25 business days |
| Structural | radome panel, pedestal casting | 10 business days | quoted per order |

Lead time runs from order confirmation, not from the fault report. Shipping is
additional and depends on destination.

## Advance replacement

Advance replacement ships the part before the faulty one comes back. It is
available:

- to **Mission** plan operators on any LRU, at no charge;
- to **Priority** plan operators on any LRU, at 15% of list price;
- to **Standard** plan operators only on a **critical** severity case, at 15% of
  list price;
- to any operator inside the 90-day commissioning burn-in, at no charge.

The faulty part must be returned within **21 days** of the replacement shipping.
If it is not, the replacement is invoiced at full list price. That invoice is
reversed if the part arrives later, minus a 10% late-return handling fee.

## RMA process

1. Raise the RMA against the service case in the portal. An RMA raised without a
   case is rejected.
2. Northbeam issues an RMA number and a prepaid return label. **The RMA number
   is valid for 21 days.**
3. Ship the part in the supplied ESD packaging. Damage caused by inadequate
   packaging is chargeable.
4. On receipt, the part is tested within 5 business days and the case is updated.

If a returned part tests as **no fault found**, a diagnostic fee of 25% of list
price applies. This is waived if the same fault code recurs on that unit within
30 days.

## Warranty

New units carry a **24-month** warranty from site acceptance. Parts supplied
under warranty are free; the labour to fit them is covered only on Priority and
Mission plans. Standard plan operators are billed labour at the published rate.

Replacement parts carry the **remainder of the original unit warranty, or 90
days, whichever is longer**.

Warranty does not cover: lightning strike, storm or physical damage, corrosion
from unmaintained radome seals, damage from unauthorised modification, or any
fault on a unit running unsupported firmware. Warranty is not extended by
goodwill and cannot be extended by support staff — extensions are a commercial
decision made by the account director.

## Consumables

Fans, filters, desiccant cartridges, and waveguide seals are consumables. They
are excluded from warranty and from the service agreement, and are billed per
order regardless of plan.
